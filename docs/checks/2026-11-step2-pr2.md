# Step 2, PR 2: emulator checks

This page records the checks run on the PR 2 build (the alert engine and the
"Finish setup" card) on an Android emulator: the setup card from first open
to complete, real SMS alerts over another app, on the lock screen and over a
running test, every way an alert ends, the light-only alert, the false-alert
cases, the largest font, TalkBack and the keyboard. It lists the two defects
found, with their screenshots and evidence, how both were fixed and
re-checked on the emulator, and what stays not tested until the test phone
is connected again.

Every result is labelled **"Tested on emulator beacon-api36, Android 16"** or
**"Not tested"**. Nothing on this page was run on the real phone.

## Defects found

As first recorded on the build of `e6aee7a`, before any fix. Both are fixed
in `53ec25e`; see [Fixes and re-checks](#fixes-and-re-checks).

| ID | Defect | Where | Screenshots | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | **Android's "Viewing full screen" panel covers the first alert and the first test.** The first time Beacon hides the system bars (plan ruling 1), Android shows its own panel: "Viewing full screen. To exit, swipe down from the top of your screen", with a "Got it" button. It covers the top card ("New message" and the app's name, or "This is how your alerts will look"), dims the rest of the screen to half brightness (the Yellow phase reads `#7F6B05`, not `#FFD60A`, and Stop turns grey), and takes the window focus (`mCurrentFocus=ImmersiveModeConfirmation`), so keyboard and TalkBack focus start on the panel, not on Stop. A tap on Stop still ends the alert. The panel comes back on every alert and test until someone taps "Got it", which Android saves for the whole phone (`immersive_mode_confirmations` becomes `confirmed`), so after that it never shows again for any app. | The first real alert (over the launcher and over the lock screen) and the first test overlay, on a phone where "Got it" was never tapped | [16-d1-panel-over-launcher-colour](step2-pr2/16-d1-panel-over-launcher-colour.png), [17-d1-panel-over-launcher-black](step2-pr2/17-d1-panel-over-launcher-black.png), [46-d1-panel-over-test](step2-pr2/46-d1-panel-over-test.png) | The alert screen shows its title and the app's name; Stop gets focus first; a deaf senior isn't asked to read system text mid-alert |
| D2 | **A real alert that starts while the screen is off ends at once.** The alert window opens, the screen turns on, and the alert ends 0.1 to 0.3 seconds later, with the light and the wake lock. Seen four times out of four: three with the lock screen showing, one with no lock screen. The event log shows the cause: the window is created while the display is still asleep, so Android pauses it at once with `userLeaving=true` (`wm_pause_activity … userLeaving=true,sleep`). A pause marked "user leaving" calls `onUserLeaveHint()`, which `AlertActivity` treats as the Home button and so ends the alert (`wm_finish_activity` 1 to 3 ms after `wm_on_top_resumed_lost_called`). With the screen already on, the same alert stays. A light-only alert (no window) keeps running with the screen off. Likely fix, not tried: start the window with `Intent.FLAG_ACTIVITY_NO_USER_ACTION`, which tells Android the start isn't a user action, or don't end the alert from a pause the system made for sleep. The Robolectric tests can't show this, because they have no sleeping display. | Every real alert with the alert window, when the screen is off: the main case, a phone asleep on the table | [18-d2-asleep-locked-alert-gone](step2-pr2/18-d2-asleep-locked-alert-gone.png) | Spec section 1, "Done means" 2: alerts work with the phone locked, asleep and on battery |

- `AlertActivityTest.launchIsNotAUserAction`: the intent carries
  `FLAG_ACTIVITY_NO_USER_ACTION` and `FLAG_ACTIVITY_NEW_TASK`.
- `AlertActivityTest.userLeavingWhileScreenOffKeepsTheAlert`: with the

  on first and still ends the alert.
- `OverlayLogicTest.barStripsStayBlackDuringTheColourPhase` and
  `blackPhaseIsBlackEverywhere`: the regions the blink paints, in portrait
  and landscape. Robolectric gives a window no system-bar insets, so the
  drawing rule is tested as plain geometry, and the real strips were checked
  on the emulator.
- `SystemBarsTest`, which checked only the swipe behaviour of the hidden bars
  (Robolectric cannot see whether bars are hidden), is removed with
  `HideSystemBars` (D1).

`launchIsNotAUserAction`, `userLeavingWhileScreenOffKeepsTheAlert` and
`barStripsStayBlackDuringTheColourPhase` failed before the fixes and pass
after them; `blackPhaseIsBlackEverywhere` is a guard that passes either way.
The full suite was 219 tests with no failures and no compiler warnings.

## Observations (not defects)

- **O1. Messages' heads-up sits over the top of the alert.** For about 5 to
  8 seconds after the SMS (seen at 4.5 s, gone by 8.5 s), Messages' own
  heads-up notification, with the sender and the message text, covers most
  of the "New message" line. On the first build, where the bars were
  hidden, the status bar came back with white icons on the flash colour
  while it showed
  ([22](step2-pr2/22-launcher-alert-heads-up-colour.png),
  [23](step2-pr2/23-launcher-alert-heads-up-black.png)); after the D1 fix
  the bars are always there, on black
  ([49](step2-pr2/49-fix-d1-first-alert-heads-up.png)). Then the alert
  screen is whole ([24](step2-pr2/24-launcher-alert-colour.png)). On the
  lock screen no heads-up showed. This comes from Android and Messages, not
  from Beacon: Beacon never shows or reads the content. With TalkBack on,
  the system also reads that notification ("Message. 555-1234, TalkBack
  test") before Beacon's title.
- **O2. The "Display over other apps" button opens the list of apps.**
  Beacon asks for its own page (`ACTION_MANAGE_OVERLAY_PERMISSION` with
  `package:dev.beacon`), but Android 11 and later ignore the app's address
  for this page and open the list of all apps
  ([07](step2-pr2/07-setup-overlay-page.png)). The person taps "Beacon",
  then the switch ([08](step2-pr2/08-setup-overlay-beacon-page.png),
  [09](step2-pr2/09-setup-overlay-granted.png)). Spec section 9 now says
  so.
- **O3. The battery steps don't match stock Android 16's wording.** The
  steps line for phones other than Xiaomi says "Tap Battery, then
  Unrestricted." On this emulator, App info has no "Battery" row: it is
  "App battery usage", then "Allow background usage", then "Unrestricted"
  ([11](step2-pr2/11-setup-battery-app-info.png),
  [12](step2-pr2/12-setup-battery-usage-page.png),
  [13](step2-pr2/13-setup-battery-options.png),
  [14](step2-pr2/14-setup-battery-unrestricted.png)). The spec already says
  other phones are not tested; the Xiaomi steps are a phone check.
- **O4. The battery steps sit above the button.** The steps line is part
  of the row's text, under the reason and above "Open settings", as the
  plan laid it out. Spec section 9 said "steps under the button"; it now
  says above.
- **O5. A keyboard-focused Stop is drawn lighter.** With keyboard focus,
  Stop's label and icon turn `#3D3D3D` on `#FFFFFF`, which is 10.86:1,
  still above 7:1 ([44](step2-pr2/44-keyboard-focus-on-stop.png)).
- **O6. TalkBack speech is longer than the test**, as in PR 1. The queued
  countdown is "3", "2", "1" at each second, but this speech engine was
  still saying "1" about 5 seconds after the overlay had closed.
- **O7. Messages' own ongoing notification is ignored.** The debug log
  showed `Ignore(reason=ONGOING)` for a Messages notification posted while
  an SMS arrived, which is rule 2 working on a real app.

## How each check was done

- **Beacon's decisions:** the debug build logs only the classifier's
  decision (`Alert(source=SMS)`, `Ignore(reason=PAUSED)`), read with
  `adb logcat -s Beacon:D`.
- **When an alert runs:** `dumpsys power` lists the `beacon:alert` wake
  lock while it is held, and its history gives each acquire and release to
  the millisecond. Each "ended at" time on this page is the release.
- **Which window is in front:** `dumpsys activity activities`
  (`topResumedActivity`, `mKeyguardShowing`), `dumpsys window`
  (`mCurrentFocus`), and `dumpsys activity recents` for Recents. Lifecycle
  events came from `logcat -b events` (`wm_*`).
- **The blink:** several screenshots about 300 ms apart, reading the
  background pixel to keep one colour phase and one black phase.
- **System bars:** on the first build, the screenshots plus
  `dumpsys window windows`, which listed the alert window's "Requested
  non-default-visibility types: statusBars navigationBars". After the D1
  fix, pixel colours read from the full-size screenshots at the strips'
  edges and on the bar icons.
- **The setup card:** granted with taps on the emulator's own Settings
  pages, so the `adb` fallback in the plan wasn't needed. Later checks
  switched overlay access with `appops set dev.beacon SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW
  deny` and `allow`.
- **Screen off and locked:** the emulator's lock screen was disabled, so a
  swipe lock screen (no PIN) was turned on with `locksettings set-disabled
  false` for checks 4 and 5. The screen was turned off with
  `input keyevent KEYCODE_SLEEP` and checked with `dumpsys power`
  (`mWakefulness=Asleep`).
- **Endings:** Stop with `input tap`, Back, Home and the power button with
  `input keyevent KEYCODE_BACK`, `KEYCODE_HOME` and `KEYCODE_POWER`. The
  timer by waiting.
- **Largest font and landscape:** `settings put system font_scale 2.0`;
  landscape with `accelerometer_rotation 0` and `user_rotation 1`, gesture
  navigation.
- **TalkBack:** as in PR 1. TalkBack's "Log output level" was set to
  VERBOSE in its developer settings, focus was moved by explore-by-touch
  through the emulator console (`adb emu event mouse`), and this page
  quotes the utterances TalkBack queued (`Pipeline: execute() …
  action=SPEAK`). Taps that start a test or press Stop were sent with
  `input tap`, which reaches the app directly.
- **Keyboard:** `input keyevent KEYCODE_TAB` until the Test alert button
  had focus (`uiautomator dump`, `focused="true"`), then `KEYCODE_ENTER`,
  then another dump.

### Not tested on the Redmi 15C 5G, Android 16

These stay "Not tested" until the test phone is connected again:

- **WhatsApp detection**, including WhatsApp's own notification layout.
- **Own replies** typed into a notification (plan ruling 7).
- **WhatsApp's backup pattern** (the ongoing backup notification).
- **Alerts with the phone locked, asleep and on battery**: a real
  cable-out test, as in Step 1. D2's fix passes on the emulator, but the
  emulator reports itself as charging, so its checks say nothing about
  Doze or battery restrictions.
- **HyperOS's listener reconnect** when Beacon is opened (plan ruling 9).
- **The camera light and the heat stop**, including the overlay's "Off
  while the phone is hot" line.
- **The real heat listener.** Robolectric refuses it, so only the
  controller's 30-second heat poll (the fallback when the system refuses
  the listener) is covered, and only by unit tests.
- **The hourly wake-lock renewal** of an "Until I stop it" alert past
  60 minutes.
- **The camera lookup warm-up on a cold start**: whether the first alert
  after the process starts waits for the camera lookup.
- **An SMS app that posts with `when == 0` and no MessagingStyle.** Every
  re-post of such a notification has time 0, so a second message in the
  same notification would be read as already handled.
- **TalkBack and the largest font on the phone**, including PR 1's D6 and
  the flash screens with HyperOS's bars on their black strips.
- **The Android 11+ notification-access detail page on HyperOS.**
- **HyperOS's "Display over other apps" and battery pages**, and whether
  the Xiaomi steps ("Tap Battery saver, then No restrictions.") match.
- **HyperOS's system bars over a flash**: whether its status bar and
  navigation bar keep their dark style with white icons on the black
  strips, and how the messaging apps' heads-ups sit over the alert there
  (O1).
- **Right-to-left for the setup card and the alert screen.** Not checked
  on the emulator either.
- **A quick second tap on Stop** that lands on the screen behind (plan
  ruling 11).

Telegram and WhatsApp Business stay **Not tested**.
