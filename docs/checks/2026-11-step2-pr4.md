# Step 2, PR 4: emulator checks

This page records the checks run for PR 4 (PR 2's last two carry-overs) on
an Android emulator: the stock Android 16 path from Beacon's App info page
to "Unrestricted", read screen by screen with `uiautomator dump`; the first
new battery steps on the setup card, followed label by label on the PR 4
build; the row at font 2.0; and what tapping the "Allow background usage"
switch instead of its words does (O2). Fix round 0 reworded the steps to
steer away from that switch; they were then followed word for word from
"Optimized" and from "Restricted" (checks 9 to 13). The other half of PR 4, the camera
light's refusal reaching the alert controller, can't be made on the
emulator and has unit tests only (the plan's ruling 4). The page ends with
what stays not tested and the settings changed and restored.

Every result is labelled **"Tested on emulator beacon-api36, Android 16"** or
**"Not tested"**. Nothing on this page was run on the real phone.

## Observations (not defects of PR 4)

- **O1. Back from App info can land in another Settings page.** In check
  1, Beacon's App info opened inside a Settings task that was already
  open (a Date & time page left from PR 3's checks), because Beacon starts
  each settings page with `FLAG_ACTIVITY_NEW_TASK` and App info belongs to
  the Settings task. Back from App info then showed that Date & time page,
  not Beacon. With no Settings task open (check 3, after
  `am force-stop com.android.settings`), App info opened in a task of its
  own and Back returned to Beacon. This is PR 2's behaviour and applies to
  all three setup rows; PR 4 doesn't change it. Whether a person who used
  Settings before tapping "Open settings" can get lost this way is open for
  the owner.
- **O2, fixed in fix round 0. "Allow background usage" is two targets in
  one row.** Its words open the page with "Unrestricted"; the switch at its
  end turns background use off, which makes Beacon "Restricted", the
  opposite of what the row asks for
  ([09](step2-pr4/09-switch-tapped-off.png)). The first new steps ("Tap App
  battery usage, then Allow background usage, then Unrestricted.") named
  only the label, so a person could tap the switch. They now name the
  words, say "not its switch", and cover a phone where background use is
  already off, whose next page greys out "Unrestricted" until its own
  switch is turned on ([11](step2-pr4/11-restricted-next-page-switch-off.png)).
  Checks 10 and 11 follow them from both states.

## How each check was done

- **Reading the screens:** `uiautomator dump`, pulled and read for each
  node's text, content description, resource id, bounds and checked
  state. `/sdcard/ui.xml` holds each dump.
- **Taps:** on Home, `input tap` on the "Open settings" button's bounds
  from the dump. In Settings (checks 3, 10 and 11), a small script found
  the node whose text was exactly the next label in the steps and tapped
  its centre, so each label had to be on the screen word for word. For
  "turn it on" (check 11), the next page's switch was read from the dump
  (`android:id/switch_widget`, checked or not) and, when off, tapped at its
  centre.
- **"Restricted" for check 11:** made the way a person could, by tapping
  the "Allow background usage" switch on the "App battery usage" page
  (App info opened with `am start -a android.settings.APPLICATION_DETAILS_SETTINGS`);
  `RUN_ANY_IN_BACKGROUND` then read `ignore`.
- **The grant:** `dumpsys deviceidle whitelist`, which lists `user,dev.beacon,10213`
  once "Unrestricted" is chosen. That list is what
  `PowerManager.isIgnoringBatteryOptimizations` reads. Background use
  with `appops get dev.beacon RUN_ANY_IN_BACKGROUND`.
- **The Battery row's state:** screenshots of the row's ON/OFF pill. The
  pill's own semantics are cleared and the dump doesn't carry Compose's
  state description, so the dump can't show it.
- **Font 2.0:** `settings put system font_scale 2.0`, Beacon brought to the
  front, the page scrolled with `input swipe`; then back to 1.0.
- **Where Back goes:** `dumpsys activity activities` (`topResumedActivity`)
  after each Back.

## Unit tests and lint

- First round: `./gradlew :app:testDebugUnitTest --rerun-tasks` at
  `82ff760`, run twice: 328 tests each time, no failures, no compiler
  warnings, and the same four "tagged … to remove other declarations but
  no other declaration present" merger warnings as on `main`.
  `./gradlew :app:lintDebug` there: 0 errors, 20 warnings.
- Fix round 0: the same command at `11160cb` and again at PR 4's final
  app code, each run twice: 324 tests each time (313 before PR 4, 11 new),
  no failures, no compiler warnings, the same four merger warnings. The
  first two runs were made with the three extra Android jars (Android 10,
  12 and 13) moved out of Robolectric's folder; neither run downloaded
  them again, and the jars were then moved back.
- `./gradlew :app:lintDebug` after fix round 0: 0 errors, 20 warnings, the
  same count as on `main`. None is about PR 4's code; the three in
  `strings.xml` (`PrivateResource` for `state_on` and `state_off`,
  `PluralsCandidate` for the speed's "blinks") were there before.
- The new tests:
  - `TorchBlinkerTest`: `aRefusalCallsOnRefusedOnce`,
    `aNormalEndNeverCallsOnRefused`, `aStopNeverCallsOnRefused`.
  - `AlertControllerTest`: `aRefusedLightStopsTheHeatPoll`,
    `aRefusalAfterTheAlertEndedChangesNothing`,
    `aRefusalOfAReplacedTestChangesTheNewAlertNothing`.
  - `TestAlertOverlayCameraTest`: `cameraLineSaysBusyWhenLightRefused`,
    at font 2.0 with the overflow check.
  - `BatteryStepsTest` (plain JUnit): `xiaomiKeepsItsStepsOnEveryVersion`
    (three letter cases), `otherPhonesFollowTheAndroidVersion` (API 29 to
    37, four other makers' names), `onlyTheExactMakerNameCounts`.
  - `HomeScreenTest` (Robolectric, default Android 16):
    `batteryStepsOnThisPhone` (the switch-safe steps on the card) and
    `android10BatteryStepsFitAtLargestFont`.
- Changed tests: `OverlayLogicTest.cameraLine` checks the refused line
  too; `aRefusedLightStopsTheHeatPoll` and `heatStopsLightAndKeepsScreen`
  check `lightRefused`; `HomeScreenTest.batteryStepsFollowTheMaker` was
  replaced by `batteryStepsOnThisPhone`, and the existing
  `setupCardNoOverflowAtLargestFont` now draws the switch-safe steps, the
  longest, at font 2.0.
- Removed in fix round 0: `SetupCardStepsTest`, which ran the card at
  API 29, 31, 33 and 36 with `@Config(sdk = …)`. Its first run downloaded
  three Android jars from Maven Central into
  `~/.m2/repository/org/robolectric/android-all-instrumented/` (122, 139
  and 160 MB); no test asks for them now.

### Not tested on any device

- **The battery steps on Android 10 to 15.** The wordings for Android 10
  and 11, 12 and 12L, and 13 come from Android's own Settings source
  (AOSP's `platform/packages/apps/Settings`, at each version's tagged
  releases), as do Android 14 and 15's, which share Android 16's. No
  emulator image for those versions was run.
- **Android 13 and 14 builds from before their wording changed.** Early
  Android 13 named the row "Battery", and Android 14 before a 2024 update
  had "Unrestricted" on the "App battery usage" page itself. Beacon shows
  each version's last wording, so on those builds one label won't match.
- **Whether Android 10 and 11 put "Battery" under "Advanced"** on a given
  phone: the source collapses App info after six rows, so it depends on
  which rows that phone shows.
- **Other makers' Settings** (Samsung, OnePlus, Motorola and others),
  whose labels may differ from stock Android's on any version.
- **The switch on Android 14 and 15.** The Settings source shows the same
  "Allow background usage" row and page as Android 16, so the switch-safe
  steps should apply, but they weren't followed there.
- **Android 12 and 13's battery page has no switch** (three radio buttons
  in the Settings source), and **Android 10 and 11's** "Background
  restriction" row asks before restricting; neither was seen.
- **The camera light refusing** on any device (the plan's ruling 4).
